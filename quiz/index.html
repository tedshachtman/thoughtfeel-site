The thoughtfeel quiz · one question

Think about your best friend.

There are four ways people do it. Read all four, then answer one question.

  1. 1

    A picture of your friend

    It has shape, size and color, and it feels like looking at something.

  2. 2

    Your friend’s voice

    You hear their voice in your head.

  3. 3

    Just knowing

    You just know you’re thinking about your friend. To feel this one, stare at something near you and pay attention to its color. You still know you’re thinking about your best friend.

  4. No shape.
    No size.
    No color.

    4

    A concept bubble of your friend

    It feels like looking at something, but it has no shape, size or color, and it’s obviously your best friend.

Can you do the fourth one?
