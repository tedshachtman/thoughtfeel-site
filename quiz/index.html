Do you have it?

Ten quick questions about everyday things: how lyrics come back to you, what a word on the tip of your tongue is like, how two situations can feel the same. Some of the answers are how people with thoughtfeel describe these things. Pick the one that sounds most like you.

There are no right answers. At the end, you’ll see which answers people with thoughtfeel give.
