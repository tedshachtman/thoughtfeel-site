    On the real panel

    • Panels differ by model. The two things to find first: the menu (a small tab on the left or right edge, the black border on the sides, or the flame-logo button on the console) and the Source button.
    • If it asks you to sign in, tap Continue as Guest.
    • Ask the host teacher: "Do you run the board from your laptop, or from the board's own browser?" Then use whichever they use.
    • A laptop needs its picture cable (HDMI) and its touch cable (USB) plugged in for touch to work, or one USB-C cable that does both. Touch 1 goes with HDMI 1.
    • If touch stops working, check the touch-lock button on the console first.
    • Annotate draws on top of anything. Tap the eye to use the page underneath. Closing Annotate erases everything you drew.
    • On the Whiteboard you can erase with your palm or fist (unless Palm Reject is on). Tap a pen twice for a different width.
    • Freeze holds the picture still, so you can fix something on the laptop without the class watching.
    • Bringing your own laptop? Bring a USB-C to HDMI adapter. If the school network blocks the site, the lesson still works on paper.
    • Older rooms may have an ActivBoard instead: a projector, a board you write on with a pen, and ActivInspire on the teacher's computer. The website just runs in that computer's browser.

    Practice only. Nothing you do here is saved or sent anywhere.
