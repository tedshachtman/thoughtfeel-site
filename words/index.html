Ten Spanish words · 5 minutes

How many will stick?

Learn ten Spanish words with thoughtfeel, a sense for concepts. Tomorrow, take a two-minute test and find out how many stuck.

Turn your sound on, and find a spot where you can speak out loud.

People with thoughtfeel report that a word’s sounds have a feel of their own. For each word, you’ll:

  1. Hear it, and notice the feel of its sounds.
  2. Hold that feel while you say out loud what the word means.
  3. Then the reverse: hold the feel of the meaning, and say the Spanish word.
